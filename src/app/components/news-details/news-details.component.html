A last-minute goal from El Moutaraji gave Wydad AC a crucial 2-1 victory in their opening Champions League match against ES Tunis at Mohamed V Stadium. The dramatic win puts the Red Castle in a strong position in Group B as they begin their campaign to reclaim the continental title.

Wydad started the match with intensity, dominating possession in the early stages. Their pressure paid off in the 23rd minute when Jabrane converted a penalty after Attiyat Allah was brought down in the box. The home side continued to create chances but couldn't extend their lead before halftime.

ES Tunis came back stronger in the second half and equalized in the 67th minute through a well-worked counter-attack finished by Ben Romdhane. The visitors appeared content with a draw as the match entered the final stages.

Coach Regragui made some tactical substitutions, bringing on fresh legs that would prove decisive. In the 89th minute, substitute El Moutaraji received a brilliant through ball from El Kaabi and calmly slotted past the goalkeeper, sending the packed Mohamed V Stadium into raptures.
